Applied throttle/brake/steering are checked against the component's physical inputs. Independent neutral runs establish equal pre-stimulus trajectories. Air cases must remain unsupported, fall under gravity and match the neutral trajectory: the stock profile has no airborne drive/steering authority. Held/released values and finite states are recorded in15CSV files.

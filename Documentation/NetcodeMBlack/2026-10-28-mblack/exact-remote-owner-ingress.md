# ExactRemote acquisition service and owner readiness

This preparatory source lot separates the transport handle from the physical input producer. `FExactRemoteInputIngress::Create` constructs only immutable binding metadata and a lifetime gate. At a canonical worker boundary, `CreateProducerForCurrentOwner` constructs the existing `FExactRemoteInputProducer` once and returns its owner-facing adapter. The transport can only submit immutable frames and inspect the service state/binding. Submission is closed before binding and permanently after retirement. A new epoch requires a new service.

The existing producer supplies its single bounded64-frame pending/history storage, continuity checks, source identity and poll fence. There is no second buffer, source-kind rewrite, synthetic neutral/held frame, baseline invention or test-scenario append path. `Accepted` means acquisition retention; it does not acknowledge a physical transition or completed publication. The adapter pins every poll/lifecycle/baseline/readiness operation to its construction thread. Ingress submissions serialize with retirement; the source is destroyed by its owner even when another thread retains the acquisition service.

`IInputProducer::QueryFrameReadiness` adds an admission hint with `Ready`, `Waiting` and `Rejected` results. Ordinary producers retain their existing synchronous behavior by default. ExactRemote reports a missing next frame as `Waiting`, invalid continuity or lifecycle/address state as `Rejected`, and an explicitly received valid next frame as `Ready`. Repeated inspection does not freeze, consume, increment a lease, quarantine, unpause or manufacture input. The caller must still complete the ordinary poll/install/step/publication transaction.

The portable suite adds readiness, distinct acquisition/owner threads, foreign pre-first-poll calls, immutable leased input with concurrent future arrivals, permanent retirement and submit/destruction race coverage.

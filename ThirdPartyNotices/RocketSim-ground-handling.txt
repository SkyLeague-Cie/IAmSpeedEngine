RocketSim ground tire/drive handling port

Upstream: https://github.com/ZealanL/RocketSim
Revision: c2baacb8f4b441dd8505e63c2aeb5a1679b60b02
Source mapping: src/Sim/Car/Car.cpp (_UpdateWheels), src/RLConst.h, src/Sim/btVehicleRL/btVehicleRL.cpp (calcFrictionImpulses/applyFrictionImpulses).
Bilateral damping: libsrc/bullet3-3.24/BulletDynamics/ConstraintSolver/btContactConstraint.cpp resolveSingleBilateral.
This adapts those laws to existing IAS force/inertia/contact APIs. It does not replace the integrator, contacts, CCD, suspension, car geometry, COM or clock. Static-ground response is the scoped validation target; dynamic-ground equivalence is not claimed.
